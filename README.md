# Group6
This app is basically a onboarding uni students best friend, it helps uni students based on what they want to do in their career and help them pick the course and major + minor, considering their lifestyle and also their personal economy. Once they select the course and subject, the app will also help the user plan out their course study plan not just for the course but for the actual subject as well, the student can import the subject outline into the chatbot assistant and the AI will actually suggest a recommended study plan for the subject as well.

this is the project brief 
This project is a lightweight, single-purpose web application—an 'app made to be deleted'—designed to help prospective domestic and international students visually model their daily life, course roadmaps, and finances before choosing a university in NSW. Instead of forcing students to decipher dense handbooks and complex degree structures, the platform interactively maps out single and double degree pathways—highlighting prerequisite chains, term-by-term workload density, and financial milestones. By comparing real-world impacts across different academic calendar models (like USyd’s 2-semester pace versus UNSW’s 3-trimester schedule), it gives prospective students complete clarity to map their future before exporting a 1-page master decision plan and enrolling.
