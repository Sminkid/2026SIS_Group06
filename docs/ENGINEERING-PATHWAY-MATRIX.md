> If you are in the Civil and Environmental major, Mechanical and Mechatronic Engineering major, or Electrical and Electronic Engineering, you must complete the aligned 24cp specialist stream. All other majors complete CBK90011 Electives.

All 14 major selections and 19 mapped variants were exercised in the browser. The required branch stays selected, its requirement preview loads, and the four top-level branch options are absent from the unrestricted selector.
